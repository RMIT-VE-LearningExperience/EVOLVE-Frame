## (b) Not evidenced: cannot be passed yet

The roof surface renders near-black and featureless, so no channel, sump or roof-sheet trim is distinguishable at the low edge. I am not inferring they are missing. Re-capture with a lower, lit angle along the low edge, or check in the headset.

The "illustrative, load path not assessed" wording is only in the shot title; I saw no in-scene tag.

- **Niches (72, 73, 74 plus mesh checks):** all three are real recesses with tiled returns, measured at 1000/900/600 wide × 450 high, 1200 sill, 60 deep. They sit on the walls confirmed at gate 2, by thumbnail judgement. The ensuite mixer is below the niche as drawn.
- **DP06 (78, full size):** the upper stem drops between two upper windows, the sloped offset sits at the render/brick junction, and the lower stem lands left of the ground-floor window. Both stems are visibly clear of glazing in this view, and the offset falls toward the lower stem. The adjacent pipe has a visible eave return and runs to the ground.

- **Laundry bulkhead (71):** present above the overhead cupboards with a continuous junction (thumbnail only).
