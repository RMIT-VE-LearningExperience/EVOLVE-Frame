ARTIFICIAL LIGHTING REQUIREMENTS:
The lamp power density or illumination power density of artificial lighting,
excluding heaters that emit light, must not exceed:
1. In Class 1 building - 5W/m.
2. Verandah or Balcony attached to Class 1 building - 4W/m.
3. In a Class 10 building - 3w/m.
The number of lights per room may change but, the total number of wattage must not exceed the
maximum allowances per each area. Refer to the electrical diagrams for maximum allowances per
each area.
