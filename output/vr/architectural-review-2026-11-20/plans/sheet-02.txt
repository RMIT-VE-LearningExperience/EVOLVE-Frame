· All storm water to be taken to the legal point of discharge in accordance with the
relevant authorities requirements and in with A.S.3500.3 - 2021

deep excavations. This is required from any buildings footing and/or slab edge

undermining of any building and it's footing system. · Pipes shall have 150mm minimum cover to non trafficable areas and
300mm(min) to trafficable or under paths, driveways etc. · Suspended stormwater pipes must be minimum 100mmØ UPVC & installed only
with a 450mm (min) subfloor clearance between underside of bearers & finished
ground. anchors & fixing centres as approved by building surveyor. · Obtain authority inspections & approvals before backfilling. · Provide agricultural drains (ag drain) to all site cuts or batters exceeding 600mm
in height. ag drains to be connected to approved stormwater system via silt pit. · The builder/contractor shall ensure that all storm water drains, sewer pipes and
the like are located at a sufficient distance form any buildings footing and/or slab
edge beams so as to prevent general moisture penetration, dampness, weakening
and undermining of any building and it's footing system. · Provide 100mm Ø min. class 6 upvc stormwater line laid to a minimum grade of
1:100 and connected to legal point of discharge (lpod). Provide inspection pits
@9000mm ctrs min and at each change of direction. · The cover to underground stormwater drains shall not be less than
(unless specified by responsible authority requirements):
- 100mm Under soils
- 50mm Under paved or concrete areas
- 100mm Under unreinforced concrete or paved areas
- 75mm Under reinforced concrete driveways · Ground & paved areas adjacent to residence footing edge shall grade away with a

other approved methods. · A.G. grate & or spoon-drains to be connected into 100mmØ upvc stormwater

12.0 - STAIRS & BALUSTRADES
· Levels & number of steps shown are approximate only. · All staircases to have: 2030mm minimum head height. · Landing to be provided where external door still exceeds 570mm or 3 risers
above finished surface of the ground with a min. of 750mm wide landing. · Hand rails to be 865mm minimum above stair nosing and landings. · Step sizes (other than for spiral stairs) to be:
1. Risers (r) 190mm maximum and 115mm minimum.
2. Going (g) 355mm maximum and 240mm minimum (residential) or
250mm minimum (commercial/industrial)
3. 2r + 1g = 700mm maximum and 550mm minimum.
4. With less than 125mm gap between open treads. · Risers and treads to be constant in size throughout flight. · All treads and landings and the like to have non-slip finish or suitable non-skid
strip near edge of nosing. · Provide balustrades where change in level exceeds 1000mm above the surface
beneath landings, ramps and/or treads. balustrades (other than tensioned wire
balustrades) to be: 1000mm min. above finished surface level of balconies,
landings or the like, & 865mm min. above finished surface level of stair nosing
or ramp, and vertical with a 125mm maximum gap between, and · Any horizontal element within the balustrade between 150mm and 760mm
above the finished floor surface must not facilitate climbing where changes in
level exceed 4000mm above the surface beneath landings, ramps and/or treads. · Wire balustrade construction to comply with NCC 2019 Volume 2 for other
classes of buildings. 1. Stainless steel wires must be 3mm Ø and 1x19 lay. 2. Supports must be provided @ 900 ctrs, droppers or posts.
3. Wire spacing shall be no more than 80mm max. 4. Wires must be tensioned to 670 newtons and shall have
max 6mm deflection when a 2kg mass is suspended mid span
between supports. 5. Solid top rail of 40mm Ø must be provided.
6. Tensioners must be provided @ each wire and @ each
change of direction greater than 30°. 7. Pulley blocks must be provided @ each change of direction.
