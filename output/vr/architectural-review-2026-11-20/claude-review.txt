# Leichhardt VR model — independent architectural review (Claude, 2026‑11‑20)

**No blanket pass.** The envelope, storey heights, stair position and window schedule are sound. However, one ground‑floor wall is about 700 mm out, the theatre corner is misread, the 0.3.3 powder fix moves the wrong elements, and the porch and garage are the wrong building forms.

**Method and limits**
- I viewed sheets 01–26 and 32 as images; sheets 27–31 were checked by extracted text only.
- The Read tool downsamples the renders to 2000 px wide (about 21 mm/px at 1:100, 10.5 mm/px at 1:50) and I cannot crop or zoom. "Scaled" figures below are ±40–50 mm and must be re‑measured at full resolution before use.
- "Confirmed" means the figure closes against a written dimension chain or an explicit note.
- I did not run Blender. Model evidence comes from `current-model-objects.jsonl` (the 0.3.3 candidate), `build_frame.py`, `vr_powder_coordination.py`, `powder-window-audit.json`, `model-revision.json`, gallery contact sheets 01–04 and the headset shot.
- Not opened: contact sheets 05–06, individual gallery shots, `opening-coordination.json`, `opening-clearances.json`, `upper-entry-coordination.json`, `finish-contact-coordination.json`, and most of the `vr_*.py`, enclosure and learning scripts. Items that depend on them are marked "verify".
- Model coordinates: the upper west cladding face is x = 6.280 and the Elevation C cladding face is y = −0.100. Frame centrelines run x 6.425–26.945 and y 0.045–7.835.

## 1. Theatre interpretation (sheet 08, cross‑checked with 07, 14, S09 and the joist layout)

### What is certain

**a) The square is a closed, full‑height framed box, not a gap.**
- Bottom chain on sheet 08: `3000 RETREAT | 90 | 330 | 90 | 3880 THEATRE | 190`.
- Right‑hand chain: `190 | 1100 | 90 | 1920 | 90 | 420 | 90 | 3480 | 90 | 420 | 190`, which sums to 8080.
- So the NW corner holds a 330 × 420 internal enclosure with 90 mm walls on all four sides. The inner square on the drawing is the inside face of those walls.
- The west and north walls run continuously through the corner as its outer faces.
- An identical box sits at the SW corner (the "510" and "420" tags). The stated 4300 × 4500 room size includes both; clear between the boxes is 3880 × 3480.

**b) The hatched band is an overhead bulkhead ring, not a wall or a floor.**
- It is labelled "340mm DROP DOWN BULKHEAD". The dashed inner line is its edge.
- It runs along the north side (510 deep), the south side (510), the west side between the two boxes (420) and the east side (scaled at about 500, not dimensioned).
- The boxes are unhatched because they are full height.
- The grey long‑dash diagonals and the centre line are the roof hips and ridge over. They are not walls.

**c) The door.** It is 820 × 2340, in the north wall beside the box, hinged on the box side and swinging into the theatre under the hatched band.

**d) The floor.**
- The solid line 2300 from the west wall is a step. The west part is labelled "PLATFORM" and the east 2000 carries the "STEP DOWN" leader.
- The structural floor is flat there. S09 and the joist layout show no set‑down; MS‑360 recesses occur only under the two showers.
- Beam 1B7 sits exactly under the step line, 2090 from the east frame face.

**e) The north wall is not collinear with Bed 5's south wall.**
- It is 3300–3390 from the Elevation A face, against 3390–3480 for Bed 5.
- The joist layout (1190 | 90 | 1920) and the load‑bearing wall under it on S09 both agree.

### What is not certain

- **What the square is.** No legend names it. The two matching squares beside the ground‑floor ensuite are tagged "VD" in the sheet 07 chains (500 / 410), also undefined. In both places the squares flank a bulkheaded recess that carries the TV and data points on sheet 14. I read it as a non‑structural framed enclosure ending a TV‑wall recess. Do not model it as a column, pier or riser, and do not put anything inside it.
- **The step height.** No sheet gives it.
- **Which side is high.** The door swings in over the "platform" zone, which is impossible if that zone is raised right up to the door wall. The drawings do not resolve this.
- **Door against bulkhead.** The upper ceiling is 2590, so the bulkhead soffit is 2250. That is below the 2340 leaf drawn swinging under it. This is a conflict in the drawings.

7. Platform: step line at x = 24.90. Remove the 420 height. Until the height is confirmed, use one riser of 190 or less and hold the platform clear of the door swing. Tag both as assumptions in object metadata.
8. Ask Chris or the builder for the step height, which side is high, and how the 2340 door meets the 2250 soffit.

## 2. Systemic errors

**S1 — Dashed and overhead linework traced as wall and floor ends.** The theatre is the confirmed case. Audit every traced wall end and platform edge against solid‑line walls only. Bulkhead edges, roof hips and ridge, the first‑floor‑over outline and the faint overhead stair risers on sheet 07 must be excluded.

**S2 — Collinearity assumed where the chains say otherwise.**
- All four hall‑line walls upstairs were traced at px 641, including the theatre north wall, which is 90–110 mm off that line.
- Downstairs, the Bed 1 north wall was traced on the same line as the ensuite/WIR north wall (px 695). The chains put it about 700 mm further north (GF‑01).

**S3 — Three independent raster registrations.**
- `gp`, `up` and `ep` are separate two‑point pixel fits at about 22 mm/px (`build_frame.py:44-45,171`), read from 1888 px previews.
- The envelope they fit is correct: 20.52 × 7.79 m frame centrelines, both storeys stacking, which closes with the chains.
- Interior features carry 30–90 mm scatter, with local errors of 180–320 mm (powder openings, ensuite/WIR wall).
- The engineering overlay is not tied to the architectural walls it draws. This is the root of the C2(F)/W03 "clash".

**S4 — Fixing by ray test rather than by source.** The 0.3.3 approach and its "PASS" prove only that the model no longer self‑intersects. They do not show that it matches the drawings.

**S5 — Compass names rotated 90°.**
- Elevation A is the EAST elevation, B is SOUTH, C is WEST and D (the garage end) is NORTH.
- The model's "North wall" is therefore the east elevation; true north is model −X.
- This is harmless for geometry but will mis‑aim any sun or bake direction keyed to those names.

## 4. Critique of the 0.3.3 candidate (C2(F), W10, W03, ST1)

**Verdict: do not install. Correct the source registration first.**

**Where the clash comes from**
- The 0.3.2 clash is real in the model: column at x 20.831–20.920 inside the W03 opening at 20.553–21.363.
- It does not exist in the drawings. S09 shows C2(F) at the west jamb of the W03 bay. The model's own audit already records this ("S09 depicts the column at the edge of the window bay").
- The overlap comes from two opposing registration errors:
  - **W03 is too far west.** Sheet 15's plan and Elevation A put it hard against the stair‑side wall. The powder door shows the same offset.
  - **C2(F) is too far east.** Tying S09 to the stair, which sheet 08 dimensions (foot at 24.90, head at 20.90), puts the column at about x 20.70–20.75. Its east face would then be at W03's west reveal.

**What 0.3.3 does instead**
- It leaves W03 where it is wrong.
- It moves the column to 20.470, about 0.23–0.28 m west of the S09 position.
- It shifts W10 by −250 with no drawing basis. The traced W10 is centred on the 1390 landing; after the shift its west jamb is about 55–80 mm from the Bed 5 partition face, too little for jamb studs at the T‑junction.
- It then rewrites ST1, 1B2, the rim and a joist end around the invented position. The script itself calls this "illustrative".

**Source‑faithful alternative**
- W03 and the powder door move east by about 0.13–0.19 m.
- C2(F) moves west by about 0.13–0.18 m, to the W03 west jamb.
- W10 stays, or moves 0.1 m at most. Its traced east jamb (20.664) already nearly coincides with the column.
- ST1 starts at C2(F) and spans the W03 head, as S09 shows.
- 1B2 stays at the stair head, bearing on ST1.
- No structural redesign is implied; this only restores what S09 draws.

All amounts are scaled and must be re‑measured at full resolution.
