5.0 - DOWNPIPES
· Provide & install downpipes of compatible material to selected or existing gutter
material.
· Downpipes to be located at maximum 12.0m spacings & within 1.2m of any
valley junction in the roof.
· Secure all downpipes to walls with straps/brackets at maximum 1800mm centres.
· Connect all downpipes to approved stormwater system that is connected to legal
point of discharge.
6.0 - EXCAVATIONS
· Take necessary measures to ensure public safety.
· Clear the proposed building area of the site of any existing structures, vegetation &
organic material.
· Ensure excavations for strip footings,concrete slab edge & internal beams are
straight & plumb to the founding depths and sizes shown on approved dwgs & as
recommended by soil report.
· Excavations to be clean/dry before placement of reinforcement.
· The Builder / Contractor is to arrange all required inspections & approvals.
· Site cuts & batters not to exceed 45° unless otherwise specified by engineer or
soil report.
· Builder to check on site the adjoining buildings to ensure foundations & footings
of any existing structures are not undermined.
· The builder and subcontractor shall ensure that all stormwater drains, sewer pipes
and the like are located at a sufficient distance from any buildings footing and/or
slab edge beams so as to prevent genera moisture penetration, dampness,
weakening and undermining of any building and its footing system.
7.0 - STORMWATER
· All storm water to be taken to the legal point of discharge in accordance with the
relevant authorities requirements and in with A.S.3500.3 - 2021
In absence of engineer designed system the following will apply.
· The builder/contractor shall ensure that all stormwater drains, sewer pipes and the
like are to be no closer than 600mm from any building line. This must be
increased as to not to impinge on the building's foundations bearing in cases of
deep excavations. This is required from any buildings footing and/or slab edge
beams so as to prevent general moisture penetration, dampness, weakening and
undermining of any building and it's footing system.
· Pipes shall have 150mm minimum cover to non trafficable areas and
300mm(min) to trafficable or under paths, driveways etc.
· Suspended stormwater pipes must be minimum 100mmØ UPVC & installed only
with a 450mm (min) subfloor clearance between underside of bearers & finished
ground. anchors & fixing centres as approved by building surveyor.
· Obtain authority inspections & approvals before backfilling.
· Provide agricultural drains (ag drain) to all site cuts or batters exceeding 600mm
in height. ag drains to be connected to approved stormwater system via silt pit.
· The builder/contractor shall ensure that all storm water drains, sewer pipes and
the like are located at a sufficient distance form any buildings footing and/or slab
edge beams so as to prevent general moisture penetration, dampness, weakening
and undermining of any building and it's footing system.
· Provide 100mm Ø min. class 6 upvc stormwater line laid to a minimum grade of
1:100 and connected to legal point of discharge (lpod). Provide inspection pits
@9000mm ctrs min and at each change of direction.
·
The cover to underground stormwater drains shall not be less than
(unless specified by responsible authority requirements):
- 100mm    Under soils
- 50mm       Under paved or concrete areas
- 100mm     Under unreinforced concrete or paved areas
- 75mm       Under reinforced concrete driveways
· Ground & paved areas adjacent to residence footing edge shall grade away with a
slope of at least 1:20 for the first meter.
· Stormwater drains shall be a minimum 100mm Ø upvc. stormwater drain shall
have a minimum of 300mm earth cover & trafficable areas such as driveways a
minimum cover of 450mm shall be provided unless protected by concrete or
other approved methods.
· A.G. grate & or spoon-drains to be connected into 100mmØ upvc stormwater
drain via silt pit to councils satisfaction.
12.0 - STAIRS & BALUSTRADES
· Levels & number of steps shown are approximate only.
· All staircases to have: 2030mm minimum head height.
· Landing to be provided where external door still exceeds 570mm or 3 risers
above finished surface of the ground with a min. of 750mm wide landing.
· Hand rails to be 865mm minimum above stair nosing and landings.
· Step sizes (other than for spiral stairs) to be:
1. Risers (r) 190mm maximum and 115mm minimum.
2. Going (g) 355mm maximum and 240mm minimum (residential) or
250mm minimum (commercial/industrial)
3. 2r + 1g = 700mm maximum and 550mm minimum.
4. With less than 125mm gap between open treads.
· Risers and treads to be constant in size throughout flight.
· All treads and landings and the like to have non-slip finish or suitable non-skid
strip near edge of nosing.
· Provide balustrades where change in level exceeds 1000mm above the surface
beneath landings, ramps and/or treads. balustrades (other than tensioned wire
balustrades) to be: 1000mm min. above finished surface level of balconies,
landings or the like, & 865mm min. above finished surface level of stair nosing
or ramp, and vertical with a 125mm maximum gap between, and
· Any horizontal element within the balustrade between 150mm and 760mm
above the finished floor surface must not facilitate climbing where changes in
level exceed 4000mm above the surface beneath landings, ramps and/or treads.
· Wire balustrade construction to comply with NCC 2019 Volume 2 for other
classes of buildings.
1.
Stainless steel wires must be 3mm Ø and 1x19 lay.
2. Supports must be provided @ 900 ctrs, droppers or posts.
3. Wire spacing shall be no more than 80mm max.
4. Wires must be tensioned to 670 newtons and shall have
max 6mm deflection when a 2kg mass is suspended mid span
between supports.
5. Solid top rail of 40mm Ø must be provided.
6. Tensioners must be provided @ each wire and @ each
change of direction greater than 30°.
7. Pulley blocks must be provided @ each change of direction.

· These drawings shall be read in conjunction with any house energy rating (her)
report for insulation details and any other energy efficiency requirements and
shall be constructed in accordance with the stamped plans endorsed by the
accredited thermal performance assessor without alterations. ( If one report is
required for this project)
· For new additions to or re-erection of class 1 buildings, unless noted otherwise,
minimum insulation provided shall be as follows:
- Conc. slab floor construction
-
Timber floor construction (Refer to notes)
- External walls. (Refer to notes)
- Bulk insulation to ceiling. (Refer to notes)
- Door seals to all external hinge doors.
- Seal round all windows and cracks.
- Self sealing exhaust fans.
- Weather strip external doors.
- All down lights to be sealed.
Note: Any sarking material (Sisalation paper) to have a flammability index not greater
than 5.
14.0 - GLAZING
· Glazing to A.S. 1288 - 2021
· Safety glazing to be used in the following cases:-
1. All rooms - within 500mm vertical of floor level.
2. All glazing in bathrooms, ensuites, spa rooms or showers - within 2000mm
vertical from the bath base, within 500mm horizontal from bath/shower to
shower doors, shower screens and bath enclosures. (including mirrors etc.)
3. Laundry - within 1200mm vertical from floor level and/or within
300mm vertical of trough.
4. Doorways within 300mm horizontal from all doors.
5. Ensuite as for shower screens shall be "grade a" safety glass.
· Glazing including safety glazing shall be installed to a size, type and thickness
so as to comply with:
- NCC 2019 Volume 2 within a design wind speed
of not more than N3.

· Where a site allotment is located within a termite designated area the
builder/contractor shall treat the entire building area with an approved termite
control treatment that complies with relevant Australian Standards & the NCC
2019 Volume 2.
· The builder/contractor shall engage a licensed and suitably qualified contractor
to carry out control treatment.
· The builder/contractor is to provide the owner with a written certificate

3660.1.)
9.0 - BRICKWORK NOTES
· Brickwork control joints to be spaced at a max of 5.0m as per CN9 and Cement
and Concrete Association. (Note TN61) or as per mentioned in the geotechincal
report.
· All wall ties to brickwork at 600mm vertically and horizontally and within
300mm of articulation joints.
· Brickwork in gables, or over windows provide continuous cavity head flashing
to weep holes @ 1000 max ctrs. Basketball rings or other sporting equipment
are not to be attached to brickwork or building unless designed for that purpose.
· General purpose class - mortar to be 1:1:6
· Protected class (within 1km of sea) - mortar to be 1:2:9
· Exposure class - mortar to be  1: ½ :4½
· Sub floor vents to be spaced evenly at 1000mm max centres, to provide
6000mm² clear air passage per meter of wall length, & max 500mm from
corners.
10.0 - TIMBER FRAMING NOTES
· All timber to be graded in accordance with A.S. 1684.4-2021 as follows: F5
Pine, F7 Oregon and F8 Hardwood U.N.O.
· All timber framework being walls or roof to be provided with bracing as per the
requirements of AS 1684.4 - 2021. All tie downs for roof are to be supplied and
fitted in accordance with AS 1684.4 - 2021 and NCC 2019 Volume 2
requirements.
· If shower base is located at load bearing walls, shower base is not to be cut into
stud frame.
11.0 - STRUCTURAL NOTES
· These drawings shall be read in conjunction with all relevant structural
drawings/computations an all other consultants drawings/details and with any
other written instructions issued which is required for this project.
· All structural steel is to be protected from corrosion in moderate and severe
environments in accordance with table AS. 4100.
· Concrete stumps:
Up to 1.4m long shall be 100mm x 100mm (1No. H.D. wire)
1.4m to 1.8m long shall be 100mm x 100mm (2No. H.D. wire)
1.8m to 3.0m long shall be 125mm x 125mm (2No. H.D. wire)
100mm x 100mm stumps exceeding 1200mm above ground level are to be
braced where no perimeter base brickwork is provided.
· All foundations shall be inspected by the building inspector prior to pouring
concrete.
· Any variation in foundation material from that specified to be notified to the
engineer and owner.
· All reinforcement in footings and pad footings shall be inspected by the building
inspector prior to pouring concrete.
· All steel and steelwork shall be in accordance with A.S. 4100.
· All dimensions shall be checked on site prior to  fabrication of steelwork.
· All timber materials, workmanship and practice shall be in
· accordance with the Timber  Engineering Codes A.S. 1720.0, 1720.2 and
1720.4 and the Timber Framing Code A.S. 1684.1 and 1684.2.
· All timber to be partially seasoned unless noted otherwise.
· All rafters and roof members to be tied down to support structure using straps,
plates, cleats, etc. by "PRYDA" or "TECO" components.
· Builder to ensure that all work is carried out to these specifications and no
spans or sizes vary. No splices are permitted without the engineer's written
consent.
· All exposed bolts, fixing, etc. to be hot dip galvanised in accordance with A.S.
1650 to suit site exposure conditions.
· Refer soil report and structural engineer's specifications for minimum founding
depth and maximum allowable bearing pressure.

where there is more than one alarm.
MECHANICAL VENTILATION
All ensuites, bathrooms, wc, p'dr or laundries without an
openable window shall comply with the following:
Clean air use 250mm dia. exhaust fan to manufacturers
specifications.
1. An exhaust fan fitted and vented to external air via a duct or
alternative piping system.
2. Mechanical light and ventilation ducted to give 25 Litres/sec.
3. Light and fan to be connected so that they are both functioning
simultaneously.
Mechanical exhausts are required in the sanitary facility rooms (25l/s
for bathroom or sanitary compartments and 40l/s for kitchen and
laundry) discharging directly to the outside via a duct or alternative
piping system or into the roof space that is ventilated in accordance
with NCC 2019 Volume 2.

builder/contractor prior to ordering.
Actual size may vary according to the selected manufacturer and site
conditions.
External windows and doors to be flashed all round.
All window and door sizes are height x width.
Window head height to be 2100mm high or to the nearest brick course.
(U.N.O.)
All internal doors to be 2100mm high (U.N.O.)

and shall extend across the full width of the glazing panel.
The lower edge of the strip shall be located 900mm from the finished
floor level. Any contrasting line on the glazing shall provide a minimum
of 30% luminance contrast when viewed against the floor surface or
surface within 2m of the glazing on the opposite side.
