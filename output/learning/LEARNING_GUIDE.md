# Frame Lab learning revision

The house and the earlier assembly studies are preserved. Three new, detached junction scenes demonstrate relationships; none is an approved construction detail.

## Blender navigation

- Scene `01 Building - enclosure`: view layers `LEARN 01` to `LEARN 04` reveal frame, wrap, cladding and roof. This is a teaching sequence, not a prescribed construction programme.
- Scenes `03 Junction - window`, `04 Junction - wall to roof`, `05 Junction - floor edge`: frame 1 is assembled; frame 100 is exploded. Press Numpad 0 for the prepared camera. Exploded offsets are NOT installation gaps.
- Numbered collections name each layer. Object metadata explains its role and assumptions.

## Learning prompts

Window: distinguish the structural opening from the window product. Follow head, jamb and sill flashing relationships. Which interfaces must be coordinated with the wrap? Where does collected water leave? What would a reversed lap change?

Eaves: follow the roof covering toward the gutter and locate the secondary membrane beneath it. Identify fascia, soffit and battens. What still needs specifying for drainage capacity, overflow, ventilation and roof fixing?

Floor edge: trace the change from lower brick to upper foam/render. Find the conceptual transition flashing. Where are movement, support and drainage details still missing? Explain why a 413 mm sample does not authorise moving the house's 400 mm floor zone.

## Evidence and limitations

The architectural notes establish 25-degree main roof pitch, 100 mm foam/render, and 10 mm plasterboard. The subfloor layout identifies 19 mm panels and the supplier 413 mm zone. The audit records the conflicting 400 mm architectural zone. The 110 mm brick / 40 mm cavity split remains an assumption.

All junction component lengths, flashings, folds, clearances, soffit thickness, batten sizes, glass and frame sections are illustrative. No fixing schedule, structural capacity, fire, energy, condensation or weatherproofing compliance is certified. The window is generic, not W04. The upper foam system is not a selected or approved product.

General reading, not project-specific approval:

- [AGWA selection guide — flashing overlap principles](https://www.agwa.com.au/common/Uploaded%20files/AGWA/Technical/AGWA%20Guides/AGWA%20Guide%20Series-Selection.pdf): weathering overlaps and provision for drainage at openings; actual installation varies by system.
- [AGWA installation resources](https://www.agwa.com.au/AGWA/Media/VidContent/Installation.aspx): consult the selected window manufacturer's current instructions.
- [Lysaght technical downloads](https://lysaght.com/support-technical/downloads): select the current manual and product-specific details, rather than treating this generic roof as a specification.
- [Your Home — condensation](https://www.yourhome.gov.au/passive-design/condensation): membrane and drying strategy require climate and assembly consideration.

## Student website

The site uses batched exports of the saved Blender geometry, with simplified materials. Stages, cutaway, orbit controls, layer inspection and exploded junctions are educational navigation. No student account, tracking, upload or assessment data is collected. All material remains provisional until teacher review.

Do not distribute the original project PDFs through the site without confirming permission. The web package contains model exports and concise teaching notes, not copies of the drawing sets.
